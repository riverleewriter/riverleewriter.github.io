# Services

The art of communication is the cornerstone of my work. With a careful eye and an open mind, I’m here to help you organize your ideas into captivating stories that persuade, empower, and move your readers.

<a href="/contact/">Contact</a> me to discuss the unique needs of your project. For samples of my work, see my <a href="/portfolio/">Writing Portfolio</a>.

<section>

## Developmental Editing

Since 2023, I’ve worked as a freelance developmental editor for short stories, memoirs, and novels. I’m well-versed in feminist issues as well as queer literature and psychological fiction. Currently, I’m accepting manuscripts for memoir, CNF, and fiction (both short and longform prose). My rates vary depending on the scope of your project, and I offer a sliding scale to reduce financial barriers.

</section>

<section>

## Event Production

### Writers’ Craft Workshops

I have over seven years of experience in curriculum design, as well as leading group discussions that center on communication, social mobility, professional development, and writers’ craft. For in-person events, I’m based in Montreal.

### Literary & Music Events

I regularly work in partnership with both emerging and established artists in the curation of literary events and music performances. If you’re looking to put together a literary, music, or hybrid event, I’d love to hear from you.
