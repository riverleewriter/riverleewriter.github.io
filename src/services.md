# Services

A commitment to the art of communication is what lies at the heart of my work. With a careful eye and an open mind, I’m here to help you transform your ideas into captivating stories that persuade, empower, and move your audience.

<a href="/contact/">Contact</a> me to discuss the unique needs of your project. For samples of my work, see my <a href="/portfolio/">Writing Portfolio</a>.

<section>

## Writer’s Craft Workshopping

Currently, I host a recurring, online workshop called <a href="https://www.eventbrite.com/cc/sapphic-writes-literary-collective-4300663" rel="noopener">Sapphic Writes!</a> and I have over seven years of experience in curriculum design as well as leading group discussions that center on communication, social mobility, professional development, and writers’ craft. For in-person events, I’m based in Montreal.

</section>

<section>

## Developmental Editing

My area of expertise is in literary journalism, as well as psychological fiction and character-driven narratives for queer BIPOC stories. With my developmental edit, I will reverse engineer your manuscript (whether short or longform), break it down into its core elements, and provide the comprehensive feedback you need to bring your story to its fullest potential. Fees vary depending on the length and scope of your project.

</section>

<section>

## Ghostwriting

Whether you’re looking to brush up on your memoir, or hone in on your storytelling, I have a range of experience in writing composition—from personal essays to corporate communication, and literary prose. Fees vary depending on the length and scope of your project.

</section>
